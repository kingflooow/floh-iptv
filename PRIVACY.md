# Privacy policy

*English · [Deutsch](DATENSCHUTZ.md)*

As of 12 October 2026

FLOH IPTV does not collect, store or transmit personal data to its developers or to third
parties.

- **No account, no ads, no statistics**: the app has no sign-in, no ads, no analytics or crash
  reporting and sends nothing to servers of its own.
- **Stored only on your device**: your settings, favorites, the playlist link, the loaded
  channel list and the TV guide are kept only on your TV, streaming device or PC (on Windows
  in the folder `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FLOH IPTV`). *Settings → Reset the app*, uninstalling or, on
  Windows, deleting that folder removes everything.
- **Connections only to your addresses**: the app connects only to the addresses you enter or
  that your playlist contains: playlist, TV guide, streams and channel logos. Like any request
  on the internet, these servers see your IP address. Their handling of data is governed by
  your provider's privacy policy.

Questions about privacy: [Issues](https://github.com/kingflooow/floh-iptv/issues).
